## Method

For each condition and metric, each seed's 100-episode raw CSV is reduced to one scalar (the per-episode mean over that seed's 100 episodes). The cross-seed mean, sample standard deviation (n-1 denominator), and standard error of the mean (SEM = std / sqrt(5)) are computed over the 5 resulting per-seed values. The 95% CI half-width is `t_(0.975, df=4) * SEM` with `t = 2.776`.
